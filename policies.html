Payment

We accept cash on delivery only. You pay when your order arrives. There are no online payments.

Delivery

A flat delivery fee is added to every order. You can see it in your cart and at checkout before you place your order.

After you order, we will contact you to confirm your order and delivery details.

Returns & replacement

No refund is given for a product that is not damaged.

A product that is not damaged can be replaced with a different one. Message us on WhatsApp and we will arrange the replacement with you.

If your product arrives damaged, please message us on WhatsApp with photos as soon as you receive it, so we can arrange a refund or a replacement.
